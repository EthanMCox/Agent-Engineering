### Results

I tested several things for this assignment. I spent a while experimenting with the standard works and what phrases worked well when searching, as well as what thresholds included sufficiently many relevant results without also including irrelevant results. I also tried several different combinations of phrases to determine how closely they related to each other. Below are a few of the things I tried:

- Synonyms vs. antonyms: I compared words such as large, colossal, and huge with tiny, small, and miniscule. Interestingly, I found that colossal matched well with words like large and big, but not with words like small or miniscule. On the other hand, antonyms matched much more closely with large, which might indicate that colossal is disassociated enough with size that it only correlated with other words that could mean large. On the other hand, the word large is more strongly connected to size, so antonyms which also predominantly related to that were still included. 

- Spelling variations: I tested words that mean the same thing but which are spelled differently depending on region (e.g., color vs. colour). I found that the different spelling still had a very high score, so the spelling did not make a significant difference. 

- Abstract concepts/feelings vs. concrete objects: results varied depending on which words I used for the phrase, but in general, there were slight differences in correlation between abstract ideas and physical objects. 

- Words which mean different things in different contexts (e.g. bank can refer to a financial institution or the side of a river): I had mixed results on this one as well, which depended on how thorough the phrase was. Even if the phrase alluded to a particular meaning, the correlational difference was smaller than I expected. However, if there were more obvious cues, (like the words money or river when referring to a bank), then those phrases had more clear differentiation. 

- I tried chunking the standard works in different sizes, and the most effective was definitely chunking by verse, but it still sometimes had issues since it failed to understand context that can be valuable from taking multiple verses together.
